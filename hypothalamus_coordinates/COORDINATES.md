# Hypothalamus coordinates in Waxholm space

Every source mapped into the same frame (Waxholm space, mm). Offsets are each centroid minus
the Waxholm atlas centroid. Generated by `hypothalamus_coordinates.py`; full numbers in `summary.json`.

## Do they match?

On the 9 hand-traced rats (rat 2's file is atlas-derived and left out):

- **Hand traces vs. Waxholm atlas:** centroids a median 0.29 mm apart (labeler 1) and 0.24 mm (labeler 2), well under one CAMRI slice (1 mm). They match closely.
- **SIGMA registered on its own vs. Waxholm atlas:** a median 0.79 mm apart, with a consistent mean offset of (-0.09, -0.56, +0.40) mm. The y axis runs front to back, the direction CAMRI's 1 mm slices are cut, so this is the partial-slice shift seen in the per-slice Dice table.
- **SIGMA atlas vs. Waxholm atlas** (atlas to atlas): 0.41 mm apart. SIGMA's region is smaller (18.8 vs. 26.8 mm³).

Axes: x is left–right (centroids sit near 0, the midline), y front–back, z top–bottom.
Sizes along y in the rat scans are limited by the 1 mm slice spacing.
